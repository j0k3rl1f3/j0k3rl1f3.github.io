Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy describes how DCBot Stream processes data to power YouTube live-chat displays, Discord account linking, and related community tools.

1. Who operates this service

DCBot Stream is an independent project operated by the DCBot Stream creator. For privacy questions or requests, contact jackwang0108@gmail.com.

2. Data processed

  • YouTube creator authorization: OAuth access and refresh tokens stored locally on the creator's server, and the authorization scope required to read the creator's live broadcast and chat.
  • Live chat: public chat message IDs, channel IDs, display names, message text, timestamps and available author-role or highlighted-message metadata.
  • Account linking: YouTube channel identifiers, corresponding Discord user identifiers, one-time verification challenges and link status.
  • Community activity: where enabled, stream-related virtual credit balances, viewer-ticket ownership, booking records and operational audit entries.
  • Operational records: limited diagnostics and event information needed to maintain and secure the service.

3. Why data is used

We use data to read and display live chat, identify the channel account submitting a linking challenge, manage Discord-linked interactions, prevent duplicate processing or abuse, and maintain system reliability.

4. Google and YouTube API data

DCBot Stream currently requests the YouTube Data API scope https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.readonly from the livestream creator. Viewer participation in chat and Discord linking does not require viewers to authorize their Google account through OAuth. Google data is used only for the app functions described here. DCBot Stream's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

5. Sharing and public display

Messages submitted to a public YouTube livestream chat may also appear in the stream's public video overlay, including author display names and supported role labels. Data may be processed by the hosting and service providers used to operate YouTube, Discord, and Streamlabs. We do not sell personal information or use Google API data to target advertisements.

6. Storage and retention

Application databases and creator OAuth credentials are intended to remain on the creator-controlled server, not in the public website repository. Some records, such as account links, stream activity, ticket ownership and financial audit entries, may persist while they are needed for account functionality, transaction integrity or security. Live-chat display entries are temporary, though limited logs or duplicate-prevention records may remain. A precise retention schedule will be finalized before public launch.

7. Security

Access to server records and tokens is restricted to operators and authorized application components. No internet service can guarantee absolute security; suspected access issues can be reported through the contact address above.

8. Your choices and deletion requests

Viewers can request details of linked information, request unlinking of their Discord and YouTube accounts, or request deletion of eligible personal data by emailing jackwang0108@gmail.com. Please include your Discord user ID or YouTube channel URL as needed for verification, but never send your password, OAuth token or verification secret. Certain transaction or security records may need to be retained where legally required or necessary to prevent fraud.

The YouTube creator can also revoke the app's Google access at Google Account connections. Revocation stops future authorized access; existing locally stored records may require a separate deletion request.

9. Children's data

This service is not designed to solicit personal information from children. Participation is subject to the applicable rules of YouTube and Discord.

10. Updates

We may update this policy as features are enabled or data practices change. Significant changes should be reflected on this page before the affected functionality is publicly offered.
