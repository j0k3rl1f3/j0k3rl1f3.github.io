DCBot Stream

A community live-stream companion connecting a creator's YouTube live chat with Discord management tools and Streamlabs display overlays.

In development

Live chat overlays

Display YouTube live chat messages, channel role indicators, and supported highlighted messages in a live broadcast overlay.

Discord account linking

Viewers may voluntarily verify control of a YouTube channel through a live chat challenge and confirm the link from their Discord account.

Community interaction

Discord-based stream credits, interactions and viewer-session tickets are being developed. Financial conversion features are not publicly enabled.

Why Google access is requested

The stream creator authorizes DCBot Stream to read information from their own YouTube livestream and associated live chat through the YouTube Data API. The app currently requests the youtube.readonly scope and does not require ordinary viewers to sign in to Google.

DCBot Stream is an independent community project, not an official Google, YouTube, Discord or Streamlabs product.
